Game Identity / Mantra: 
A first-person audio-mystery where you must identify a sick guest at a masquerade ball by listening for a cough in the crowd.
Design Pillars:
Listen. Deduce. Act.
Genre/Story/Mechanics Summary
A Social Deduction Horror game set in a single hotel ballroom. The player is a detective trying to find Patient Zero. Without high-tech gadgets, you must rely on Social Eavesdropping (listening to conversations to catch lies) and Spatial Audio (tracking the sound of coughing or wheezing to a specific guest). As the game progresses through 3 time-based phases, the "symptoms" become louder and the guests become more hostile.
Features: 
Phase 0: Carrier (Hidden)
NPCs behave normally. Patient Zero starts here but quickly advances.

Phase 1: The Cough (Audio)
Light cough sound. Subtle audio cue playing randomly.
Behavior: Largely normal, but occasionally stops to cough.

Phase 2: The Twitch (Animation)
Subtle spasms. The "Mask" starts to crack.
Movement: Erratic. Patient Zero will occasionally twitch (a rapid 10-degree rotation left/right) or head-shake.

Phase 3: Invasion of Space (Social)
Socializing, but wrong.
Behavior: They stand uncomfortably close to other NPCs. Instead of a polite distance, they crowd the personal space of others in the group.

Phase 4: The Stare (Interaction)
Paranoia.
Behavior: Staring (subtle) at the player for a few seconds. They will turn their head or body to track the player, then snap back to normal behavior.

Infection Mechanic:
Only one Initial Patient Zero.
When Patient Zero advances a phase, they infect one random healthy NPC.
The newly infected NPC starts the progression from Phase 1.

Art Style: 
Visuals: Low-poly, unlit PSx style.
Characters: Generic masked bodies. We could distinguish them only by Color (Red Suit, Blue Dress, etc.) and Position.
Atmosphere: Dense distance fog to hide the corners of the room, forcing the player to get close to investigate.


Music/Sound: 
Concept: Patient Zero has a unique audio loop attached to their prefab (e.g., a wet, rattling breath) that plays randomly every 10–20 seconds.
